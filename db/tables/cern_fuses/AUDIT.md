## Tail derivation (fuse_kind, current_rating)
- [ ] `fuse_kind` is the CERN `Family` column (deterministic, filled 315/315),
      copied verbatim except the one plural normalized `Fuses Resettable` →
      `Fuse Resettable`. Distribution: Fuse 169, Fuse Resettable 87, Fuse Holder
      22, Fuse & Holder 16, Surge Arrester 14, Fuse Holder Cover 3, Fuse Clip 3,
      Fuse SCP 1. `Family` is far cleaner than the symbol names here, which are
      generic (`Fuse`, `Fuse No Pin`, `Fuse Holder`) and don't distinguish
      resettable from non-resettable. (`Component Type` is uniformly `Standard`;
      `Component Kind` Accessories/Standard is unreliable — many real fuses sit
      under Accessories.)
- [ ] `current_rating` copied verbatim from the CERN `Value` column (287/315
      filled; e.g. `1A-32V`, `500mA-63V`, `230VAC`). The 28 blanks are exactly
      the holders/clips/covers (Fuse Holder 22, Fuse Clip 3, Fuse Holder Cover 3)
      — they carry no current/voltage rating. Also surfaced as `value`
      (description as fallback for the blank-rating rows).
- [ ] No separate breaking-capacity / speed (FF/F/T/TT) column: those live only
      in free-text descriptions, not deterministically parseable, and absent from
      the symbol names.

## 3D model coverage
- [ ] 46/315 parts (~15%), 29 footprints, via the new fuse rules in
      tools/model_map.py (`_resolve_fuse`, wired into `resolve_from_footprint`):
      standard SMD chip-fuse / chip-PTC bodies (0402/0603/0805/1206/1210 size
      tokens, plus dimensioned `FUSRC<LL><WW>X…` resettable names) → KiCad's
      `Fuse_<size>_<metric>` chip models; Schurter 0031.7701 / 0031.8002 and
      Bulgin FX0457 cylinder PCB holders → their exact bundled holder models.
      Locked by 4 new cases in tests/test_model_map.py.
- [ ] Residual ~85% is expected, not a regression: bare cartridge bodies
      (5x20/6.3x32/10x38/6x46mm glass+ceramic — KiCad ships matching *holders*
      but no bare-cartridge model), big SMD chips (1812/2410/2920/3812 — no KiCad
      chip-fuse model above 1210), bespoke resettable PTC bodies (Bourns
      MF-R/MF-RX radial, MF-SM/NSMF/PSMF/USMF disc; Littelfuse NANO/MICRO-SMD,
      RXEF/RUEF/RHEF; BelFuse 0ZCJ; Bussmann PTS), TR5 radial fuses (Littelfuse
      466/467/468), bespoke holders/clips/covers (Keystone, Schurter OGN/3101,
      Littelfuse blocks, Bussmann/Eaton/Mersen), and all surge arresters
      (Bourns/Littelfuse/TDK/Siemens/Dexerials GDTs). Documented in
      model_map.SKIP_REASON; → human drop-folder under kicad_3dmodels/.
- [ ] Embedded part-number digit runs are never mis-read as a body size: the
      chip-token regex requires a non-digit boundary on both sides, so the
      `1206` in `FUSR_BUSSMANN_PTS120660V005` is rejected (PTS is a disc PTC, not
      a 1206 chip).
