## Tail derivation (decision: NO type-specific tail)
- [x] CERN `Transformers` is a grab-bag: mains power (BLOCK VB/PT/FL, Myrra EI/UI,
      Talema/Nuvotem toroidal, BelFuse LP, Triad, Hammond, Vigortronix), signal/
      audio (Sirio, Schaffner IT), pulse/fieldbus (Alstom FIELDTR, CERN HCRA*), RF
      balun/wideband (Coilcraft WBT, Mini-Circuits, Macom, MiniRF, Anaren), Ethernet/
      LAN magnetics modules (Halo, Pulse, Wurth), current-sense cores (VAC), and
      common-mode chokes (Wurth, Schurter).
- [x] No deterministic transformer-category dimension exists: `Component Type` and
      `Component Kind` are uniformly `Standard` (230/230 each); there is **no
      `Family` column** (unlike Fuses/Relays); `Case` is empty (0/230). The 160
      distinct `LibSymbol` names are either the vendor part
      (`XFMR_<VENDOR>_<PART>`) or generic winding-topology symbols
      (`Transformer 2xPrim 2xSec Type1`) — neither yields a clean parametric type.
      Parsing turns-ratio / inductance / VA from the free-text `Part Description`
      is explicitly out of scope per the skill. Per the port-cern-library skill,
      "no tail" is the correct outcome for a grab-bag category (cf. cern_sensors).
- [x] Schema = canonical core + adopted `pin_count` / `component_height` only. The
      `test_no_type_tail_columns` test guards against a parametric column sneaking
      in. `tags='transformer'` on every part (one row in the shared `tags` table
      each).

## 3D model coverage
- [x] 0/230 parts (0%). This is the expected LOW (zero) outcome for this category,
      not a regression. CERN's transformer footprints are 163 bespoke vendor cores
      (XFMR_<VENDOR>_<PART>) with `package`/`Case` blank; the body is encoded only
      in the vendor-series footprint name. KiCad's Transformer_THT / Transformer_SMD
      3dshapes libraries ship only a handful of unrelated vendor-specific bodies
      (Breve TEZ, Hahn/CHK EI, Lundahl, ETAL, Triad VPP16-310, Halo TG111-MSC13).
      None of those is the body of any CERN Transformers part: CERN's lone Halo is
      the TG111-E001J24RL (J24 body, not MSC13), and its Triads are CST/CSE/TY/FS/SP
      series, not VPP16. No mapping is forced; the deliberate non-coverage is
      recorded in `model_map.SKIP_REASON["XFMR vendor core"]`. These go to the human
      drop-folder under kicad_3dmodels/. No new resolver rule was added (nothing to
      lock with a model-map test).
